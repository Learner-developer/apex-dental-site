## 2. Complete Clinical Services Taxonomy

### Department 1: Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopedics *(Flagship Specialty)*
*Headed directly by Dr. Jyothi Sajjan (MDS Orthodontist)*
- **Metal Braces (Traditional & Self-Ligating):** High-grade stainless steel brackets for durable, precise correction of severe malocclusion.
- **Ceramic (Clear) Braces:** Tooth-colored brackets for aesthetic orthodontic alignment.
- **Invisible Clear Aligners:** Custom-fit, removable clear trays for discreet smile transformation.
- **Lingual Braces:** Hidden brackets attached behind the teeth.
- **Fast-Track Orthodontic Alignment:** Optimized treatment plans achieving full alignment within **7 to 11 months** (verified in clinical reviews).
- **Malocclusion Corrections:** Severe crowding, midline diastema (spacing), deep bite, crossbite, overjet/overbite, and ectopic canines.
- **Retention & Post-Braces Care:** Custom clear retainers and bonded wire retainers.

### Department 2: Root Canal Treatment & Endodontics *(Pain-Free Care)*
- **Rotary Endodontics:** Performed using the **NSK Endo-Mate DT** micro-motor system for rapid, painless canal shaping.
- **Single / Two-Sitting RCT:** Fast relief from acute toothache and pulp infection with minimal chair time.
- **Digital OPG & X-Ray Diagnostics:** Chairside digital radiographic assessment of root anatomy.
- **Re-RCT & Failed Root Canal Management:** Corrective treatment for previously infected or leaking canals.

### Department 3: Oral Surgery & Extractions
- **Painless Wisdom Tooth Removal:** Atraumatic extraction of impacted, angled, and partially erupted third molars.
- **Surgical & Routine Extractions:** Gentle removal of severely decayed, broken, or mobile teeth.
- **Pre-Orthodontic Serial Extractions:** Planned bicuspid extractions for severe crowding relief.
- **Proactive Post-Extraction Follow-up:** Comprehensive recovery protocols and doctor check-ins.

### Department 4: Prosthodontics, Crowns & Dental Implants *(Restorative)*
- **Zirconia Crowns & Bridges:** High-strength, metal-free, natural translucent aesthetic restorations.
- **Ceramic / Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal (PFM) Crowns:** Durable anterior and posterior crown restorations.
- **Dental Implants:** Permanent titanium root replacements for single and multiple missing teeth.
- **Complete Dentures (Full Mouth Rehabilitation):** Custom-crafted full dentures tailored for senior comfort, mastication, and facial aesthetics.
- **Partial Dentures (Removable & Fixed):** Flexible and cast partial dentures (RPD / FPD).

### Department 5: Cosmetic & Aesthetic Dentistry *(Smile Makeovers)*
- **Direct Composite Bonding:** Single-visit tooth-colored resin bonding for chipped, worn, or uneven teeth.
- **Midline Diastema (Gap) Closure:** Minimally invasive closure of front teeth gaps without brackets.
- **Fractured Front Tooth Cosmetic Reconstruction:** Multi-layer composite restoration of fractured central/lateral incisors.
- **Porcelain Veneers & Laminates:** Ultra-thin custom shells for complete smile redesign.
- **Teeth Whitening & Stain Removal:** In-clinic bleaching and polishing for instant brightness.

### Department 6: Periodontics & Preventive Care
- **Deep Ultrasonic Scaling:** Subgingival and supragingival tartar/calculus removal using ultrasonic scalers.
- **Dental Stain Polishing:** Air-powder and paste polishing for tobacco, tea, and coffee stains.
- **Gingivitis & Gum Bleeding Treatment:** Deep pocket debridement and antimicrobial rinses.
- **Preventive Dental Hygiene Consultations:** Oral hygiene coaching and annual maintenance checkups.

### Department 7: Pediatric & Family Dentistry
- **Child Dental Checkups:** Friendly, anxiety-free atmosphere for children.
- **Fluoride Therapy & Pit/Fissure Sealants:** Preventive protection against childhood caries.
- **Milk Tooth Fillings & Space Maintainers:** Preserving arch space for incoming permanent teeth.
- **Habit-Breaking Appliances:** Custom appliances for thumb-sucking and tongue thrusting.
