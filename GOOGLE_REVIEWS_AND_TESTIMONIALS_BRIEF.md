## 1. User Headshot & Avatar Implementation Strategy

To display authentic patient headshots on the website's review cards:

1. **Avatar Headshot Placeholder API (Pre-configured in JSON):**
   - Each testimonial in [`curated_testimonials.json`](file:///c:/Users/nisha/Downloads/Apex%20Dental%20Assets/curated_testimonials.json) has a pre-built, theme-matched avatar URL:
     `https://ui-avatars.com/api/?name={Author+Name}&background=0D9488&color=ffffff&size=128&bold=true`
2. **Trust & Verification Badges:**
   - **Badge 1:** `Google Verified Reviewer` / `Google Maps Review`
   - **Badge 2:** `Google Local Guide` (for top reviewers with high contribution counts)
   - **Badge 3:** `★★★★★ 5.0`
3. **Direct Verification Link:**
   - Every review includes its direct `google_review_url` to allow visitors to verify authenticity on Google Maps in one click.
