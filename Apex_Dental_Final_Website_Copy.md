## 5. Core Services (tabbed/accordion — all 7 departments)

### Tab 1 — Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopedics
> Led directly by Dr. Jyothi Sajjan, MDS in Orthodontics.
> - **Metal braces** — durable stainless-steel brackets for correcting moderate to severe misalignment.
> - **Ceramic (clear) braces** — same correction, tooth-colored brackets for a less visible option.
> - **Clear aligners** — removable, custom-fit trays for patients who prefer a discreet, take-out option.
> - **Lingual braces** — brackets fitted behind the teeth, fully hidden from the front.
> - **Retainers** — custom clear or bonded wire retainers to hold your results after braces come off.
> Most patients complete treatment in 7-11 months, depending on the case.

### Tab 2 — Root Canal & Endodontics
> Performed using rotary endodontic technology for faster, gentler treatment.
> - **Single or two-sitting root canal** — most cases completed in one or two visits.
> - **Digital X-ray diagnostics** — the root's exact condition is checked chairside before treatment starts.
> - **Re-treatment** — for a root canal done elsewhere that's still causing problems.

### Tab 3 — Oral Surgery & Extractions
> For wisdom teeth and extractions, handled with care.
> - **Wisdom tooth removal** — for impacted, angled, or partially emerged third molars, removed with an approach that limits trauma to surrounding tissue.
> - **Routine and surgical extractions** — for teeth too decayed, broken, or loose to save.
> - **Pre-orthodontic extractions** — planned removal of specific teeth to make room before braces, when a case needs it.
> - **Post-extraction follow-up** — a check-in call after your procedure, not just a form to sign on the way out.

### Tab 4 — Prosthodontics & Dental Implants
> Replacing missing or damaged teeth, from a single crown to a full set of dentures.
> - **Zirconia crowns & bridges** — metal-free, natural-looking restorations.
> - **Ceramic and PFM crowns** — durable options for front and back teeth.
> - **Dental implants** — titanium tooth-root replacements for one missing tooth or several.
> - **Complete dentures** — a full custom-made set for comfort and a natural bite.
> - **Partial dentures** — removable or fixed options when only some teeth need replacing.

### Tab 5 — Cosmetic & Aesthetic Dentistry
> Fixing chips, gaps, and stains without a full smile overhaul.
> - **Composite bonding** — a same-visit fix for chipped, worn, or uneven teeth.
> - **Gap closure** — closing a space between the front teeth without braces.
> - **Fractured tooth repair** — rebuilding a broken front tooth to match the rest of your smile.
> - **Porcelain veneers** — thin custom shells for a full smile redesign.
> - **Teeth whitening** — in-clinic bleaching and polishing for a brighter smile.

### Tab 6 — Periodontics & Preventive Care
> Keeping your gums and teeth healthy between bigger procedures.
> - **Deep ultrasonic scaling** — removes tartar buildup above and below the gumline.
> - **Stain polishing** — lifts tea, coffee, and tobacco stains.
> - **Gum treatment** — for bleeding or inflamed gums, before it becomes a bigger problem.
> - **Hygiene consultations** — a yearly checkup and a personalized care routine.

### Tab 7 — Pediatric & Family Dentistry
> A calm first dental experience for kids, and ongoing care as they grow.
> - **Child checkups** — a friendly, unhurried visit designed to keep kids relaxed.
> - **Fluoride treatment & sealants** — protects young teeth from cavities before they start.
> - **Milk tooth fillings & space maintainers** — treats baby teeth and holds space for adult teeth coming in.
> - **Habit-breaking appliances** — custom devices to help with thumb-sucking or tongue-thrusting.

## 11. FAQ Accordion

**How long does teeth alignment take with braces?**
Traditional orthodontic treatment often takes 18-24 months. At Apex Dental, most mild-to-moderate cases are completed in 7-11 months, depending on the individual case.

**Is root canal treatment painful?**
Most patients describe it as painless or close to it. Local anesthesia plus rotary endodontic equipment keep the procedure quick and gentle.

**Do you offer a free consultation?**
Yes — braces and clear aligner consultations are free. Dr. Jyothi Sajjan examines your case, takes any needed diagnostics, and gives you a personalized treatment plan before you commit to anything.

**How does pricing compare to other clinics?**
Patients regularly mention that treatment here costs less than at other clinics they'd considered, while still using materials like zirconia crowns and rotary endodontics. Ask for a quote at your consultation — it's free either way.

**How do you handle hygiene and sterilization?**
Every instrument is sterilized in a dedicated autoclave bay between patients, and single-use disposable barriers are used throughout.
